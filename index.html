About

"Collecting light, one frame at a time."

I'm Ethan Kang — a photographer, occasional writer, and permanent wanderer between Hong Kong and everywhere else. I chase the quiet moments most people walk past: the way golden hour folds across a brick wall, the split second before laughter breaks, the geometry of an empty street at dusk when the neon hasn't quite turned on yet.

These photos are my letters to no one in particular. Part diary, part gallery, part open door. I don't chase perfection — I chase presence. Each frame is a small prayer to the light that was there and will never be there again. Stay a while — the coffee's on the stove and the window's open.

When I'm not behind a camera, you'll find me drifting through antique streets, reading old poetry in loud cafés, or trying (and mostly failing) to keep houseplants alive. The city teaches patience — I'm still learning.

— Ethan Kang
